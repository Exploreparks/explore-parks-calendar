Angels Landing permit

  • Apply: Spring lottery on Recreation.gov
  • Official NPS permit info and dates
  • $6 non-refundable application, up to 6 people, rank up to 7 dates/times. Winners pay $3 per person. The confirmation email is your permit, so screenshot it.
  • Backup: day-before lottery on Recreation.gov, 12:01 AM to 3 PM Mountain the day before.
  • Trailhead: The Grotto, shuttle stop 6.
