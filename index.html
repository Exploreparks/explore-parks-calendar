How it works

  1. Pick a park. Each park below has its own calendar file. The rules come from official NPS and Recreation.gov pages, and every park lists its sources.
  2. Add it to your calendar. One click downloads an .ics file you can open in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.
  3. Get a nudge. Every event in the file has an alert 1 day before and another 15 minutes before. Then you go to Recreation.gov (or the park's NPS page) and book it yourself.

What this sheet covers

This page has calendar reminder files for 22 national parks: Acadia, Arches, Big Bend, Bryce Canyon, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef, Death Valley, Denali, Glacier, Grand Canyon, Grand Teton, Great Smoky Mountains, Haleakala, Joshua Tree, Mount Rainier, Olympic, Rocky Mountain, Sequoia & Kings Canyon, Shenandoah, Yellowstone, Yosemite and Zion.

What is in each file. A few parks have specific dates and times, like Zion's Angels Landing lottery, Yosemite's monthly campground release, or Haleakala's sunrise reservations. Other parks only have a simple "check the booking rules" reminder on the 1st of each month. Those are tagged [TIME UNVERIFIED], which means the time is a placeholder and not an official release time.

What it is not. It is a reminder tool only. It does not book campsites or permits, hold a spot for you, or watch for cancellations. Bookings happen on Recreation.gov or the park's own page.

Please double-check. Dates and rules change, and we can miss an update. Before you plan around any reminder, confirm the date and time on Recreation.gov or the park's NPS page. Parks marked unverified or partial have had less checking than the rest.

Free: Big Bend calendar

What it is. A reminder, not a booking. It nudges you once a month to check Big Bend National Park's campsite and backcountry permit windows.

What it covers.

  • Chisos Basin Campground (60 sites). Reservation only, with no first-come camping. About two-thirds of the sites open 6 months ahead. Sites 8-10, 12-15, 31, 37 (accessible) and 48-60 open 14 days ahead. New dates open daily at 9:00 AM Central (10:00 AM Eastern) on Recreation.gov.
  • Backcountry permits for Chisos backpacking sites, primitive roadside sites and desert backpacking. A permit is required for all backcountry camping ($10 per night). Some Chisos and roadside sites can normally be booked on Recreation.gov up to 180 days ahead. Right now, NPS says all backcountry permits are in person only at the Panther Junction Visitor Center, up to 24 hours before your first night (8:30 AM to 5:00 PM daily). So there may be nothing to book online yet.

Not covered. Rio Grande Village, Cottonwood, the group campsites, the Chisos Mountains Lodge and river permits are not in this calendar.

How to use it.

  1. Tap the button to download the file and open it in your calendar app.
  2. You get one repeating event on the 1st of every month at 9:00 AM Central, starting Nov 1, 2026. Your phone alerts you the day before and 15 minutes before.
  3. When the alert fires, open the Chisos Basin page on Recreation.gov and look at the dates you want. Check NPS for the current backcountry permit rules too.
  4. Booking happens on Recreation.gov, not here. If you already know your dates, count back 6 months (or 14 days for the 14-day sites) and set your own alarm for 9:00 AM Central that day. The monthly reminder is a nudge to look, not the exact moment a particular night opens.
